The Story

THE
MANIFESTO

Born Usongo Jeremiah Sesugh, now known to the streets and the stage as Governor Zue.

Governor Zue is a Nigerian artist who turns everyday life into music people can feel. Since 2023 he has built a catalog one release at a time, from "Polo" to "One Dance," "Parte," "Faya," and his latest record, "Oh Maria."

His sound is grounded and relatable, with rhythm you move to and words you remember. The mission is simple: stay consistent, stay real, and take the movement worldwide.
